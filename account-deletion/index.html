How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the app. Please note that this action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Before you begin:

If you wish to keep your information, we recommend backing up your data first. Go to the main menu and select Export & Import to download your files.

Steps to delete:

  1. Open the app and tap the My account icon in the top menu.
  2. Select Delete account from the menu options.
  3. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm.
